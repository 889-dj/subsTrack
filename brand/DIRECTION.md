# Brand direction: "Night Watch"

**Essence:** a calm guardian for your recurring charges. **Tone:** calm, watchful, premium (not cute).

**Chosen mark: the Panther.** A sleek black panther head with glowing cyan eyes on an indigo tile.
Why: it is a night guardian (watching over charges you forget), reads from its silhouette alone at 29px and in
black and white, is grown-up rather than cartoonish, and doubles as the in-app mascot. Search found no
subscription app using a panther. The first round (a round-faced bear) and a second round (firefly,
tarsier, wisp) were rejected as too cute/generic; see `explorations/`.

Avoid: cards/coins/wallets, orbit/moon/plain-ring metaphors (existing subscription apps use them), text inside the icon, baked glows.

Research status: Apple HIG loaded; competitor icon galleries and store pages mostly blocked or partial;
collision check came from search snippets only (not visually verified).
